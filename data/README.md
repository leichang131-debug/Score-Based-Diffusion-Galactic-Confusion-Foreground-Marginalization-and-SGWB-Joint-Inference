# Data management

The new study uses a shared simulation and preprocessing workflow. Track locked test observations and simulation provenance under [shared research evidence](../results/research/shared/README.md).

The current full-covariance baseline has a dedicated [data notes](taiji-sgwb-time-domain/README.md).

Document simulator version, physical parameters, orbit and TDI definitions, units, sampling and FFT conventions, source-selection rules, and independent population identifiers. Track small public fixtures only when their provenance and distribution rights are clear.

Bulk raw and processed data are ignored in this repository. Register external artifacts and checksums in `results/manifests/`.
